(05)Why this works

Verified, not vibes.

Anyone can say “AI.” Here's what our systems actually do differently.

Operator judgment, not tool enthusiasm.

The hard part isn't the AI — it's knowing what to build. One growth plan cross-checked 2,077 keywords against 28 real customer call transcripts, so every page matched what buyers actually say.

Systems that check their own claims.

Our builds verify against the source of truth. One ops system validated every “refund saved” claim against the payment platform itself — and caught saves that had quietly refunded. Integrations are idempotent by design: re-runs self-heal instead of duplicating.

Root cause, then handover.

When something breaks, we fix the data AND rebuild the workflow so it can't recur — then document it node-by-node so your team owns it. You keep the system, not a dependency on us.
