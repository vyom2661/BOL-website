Where we started

Zero authority. New domain, 0 organic rankings, no crawl data to build on.

Thin, duplicative content. Only 4 flagship airports had bespoke content; the other 64 URLs rendered identical boilerplate — thin and a ranking liability.

A latent bug. Airports without a database row returned “Airport not found,” so most airport pages never showed their static content at all.

(02)

What we did

Strategy first. A full 247-keyword content strategy on DataForSEO data — 140k+ monthly search volume identified at an average keyword difficulty of ~15, across five content pillars with ten prioritised quick wins.

Rebuilt the marketing pages. Airports, Services, Pricing, Corporate and Blog redesigned to a luxury-editorial design system, then hardened for search and AI discovery.

The heavy lift — all 64 remaining airports, written from scratch. Unique, fact-grounded editorial (~2,100–2,400 words each), correct locale conventions, every meta title and heading unique. Result: 68/68 airports content-complete, zero boilerplate, zero “not found.”

Reusable SEO + AI-SEO infrastructure. Absolute Open Graph images, AggregateRating JSON-LD, and a blog renderer upgraded for tables, internal links, TL;DR and sources blocks — so content is machine-extractable by Google and AI assistants.

Social proof. A reusable TrustStrip (4.9 rating / 2,000+ reviews) and mini-testimonials placed across service, airport, pricing and blog pages.
