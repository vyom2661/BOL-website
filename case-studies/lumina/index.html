(01)

What the data showed

We pulled and scored 2,077 qualified keywords in the inventory and demand-planning niche — fresh DataForSEO data, US + UK — then cross-checked every term against 28 customer call transcripts, so the plan matched the words real prospects use, not just what a tool said had volume.

The niche was underbuilt. 1,270 reachable keywords sit in the easy band — rankable in weeks, provided the pages exist. The job wasn't to invent a strategy; it was to ship the pages before anyone else noticed.

Three surfaces carried the opportunity. A glossary (340k/mo of definitions ranking as dictionary stubs), competitor-brand searches (108k/mo, highest intent in the niche), and lead-magnet templates + calculators (75k+/mo, every visitor the ICP).

(02)

What shipped

The build went from 45 static pages to 96 — 67 net-new commercial surfaces, every one pre-rendered with the right structured data.

A 61-entry glossary · ~340k/mo. The highest-volume surface and the internal-linking spine for everything else — six categories, 600+ words per entry, cross-linked into the calculator and templates.

Competitor-switch pages · ~108k/mo. NetStock Alternatives + comparison pages on reusable templates — then, beyond scope, pointed at Inventory Planner and Prediko too, with hub pages to parent them.

Tools & templates. A free auto-totalling Purchase Order template (email-gated) and an interactive Safety-Stock calculator — the list-building surface, every visitor the ICP mid-task.

Seven schema types + the connective tissue. DefinedTerm, FAQPage, Article, HowTo, ItemList and more; an internal-linking pass across all 96 URLs, a nav restructure, and 17 brand OG images to close every 404'd social card.

(03)

Honest about the line

This is a foundation delivered, not traffic banked. The pages are live and indexable; measurement is pending the client's Search Console verification — so the numbers are a projection from the keyword data, and we say so.

A sober 6-month capture. 3–8% of addressable volume — roughly 13,000–34,000 organic sessions/month to the new surfaces, weighted to the glossary.

Delivered vs promised. 17 items scoped, 14 shipped as planned, 9 added beyond scope and none billed extra; the 3 not closed are gated on the client or on production traffic, not on us.
